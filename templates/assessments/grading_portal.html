Terminal workflow
  1. Teacher is assigned to class + subject.
  2. Record CA/class work or enter the final Class Score /30.
  3. Generate, write or print the examination.
  4. Mark the papers manually or online.
  5. Enter Examination /70.
  6. System calculates Final /100, grade and remark.
  7. Generate and approve end-of-term report cards.
