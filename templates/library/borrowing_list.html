How Borrowings Work

This page tracks all books currently borrowed from the library. Each record shows the book, borrower (with type badge: Student or Staff), borrowed date, due date, and current status (Active, Overdue, or Returned). Use the search box below to filter borrowings by book title or borrower name. Click "New Borrowing" to lend a book, or use the "Return" or "Lost" buttons to update the borrowing status.
