How Fee Preparation Works

This page allows you to prepare fees for students before they are invoiced. Select an Academic Term and a School Class, then click "Prepare Class Fees" to generate fee records for all students in that class. The Class Status Cards show preparation progress for each class. Use the search box below to filter the fee preparation list by student name or status. Once fees are Prepared, they can be Approved and then Invoiced.
