How Student Fees Work

This page shows all prepared student fee records. The KPI cards at the top display the total count of fees by status: Draft, Prepared, Approved, Invoiced, and Cancelled. The summary cards show the Total Base Amount, the Total New Student Class Add-ons, and the Total Final Amount. For each new student, the Base Amount is the class base fee, Add-ons contains only the new-student add-ons configured for that student's class, and Final Amount is Base Amount + Add-ons. Use the Status Filter and Search box to find specific fee records. Select multiple Prepared fees and click "Approve Selected" to approve them in bulk.
