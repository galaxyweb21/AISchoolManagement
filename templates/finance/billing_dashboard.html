How Billing Dashboard Works

This dashboard shows your school's financial collections at a glance. The KPI cards summarise Total Billed, Revenue Collected, Outstanding Balance, and Collection Rate. The Accounts Receivable Ledger lists every invoice with its status. Use the search box and status filters to narrow down, and click "Record Payment" on any unpaid invoice to process a payment.
