How Fee Categories Work

Fee categories define the types of fees charged to students (e.g., Tuition, Sports, Library). Each category can be Recurring (charged every term) or Optional (added manually to specific students). Use the search box below to filter categories by name or type. Click "Add Category" to create new fee categories for your school.
