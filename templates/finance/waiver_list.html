How Fee Waivers Work

Fee waivers allow you to apply discounts or exemptions to student fees. Each waiver specifies the student, reason, type (Percentage or Fixed Amount), value, and the term it applies to (or "Standing" for ongoing waivers). Waivers can be Active or Inactive. Use the search box below to filter waivers by student name, reason, or type. Click "Add Waiver" to create a new waiver, or "Edit" to modify an existing one.
