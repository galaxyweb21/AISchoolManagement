How School Dashboard Works

This dashboard manages your school's academic years and terms. Each academic year can have multiple terms (e.g., Term 1, Term 2, Term 3). Click on a year to expand and view its terms. Use the "Add Term" button inside each year to create new terms, or "Edit" and "Delete" to manage existing ones. The "School Settings" button allows you to update your school's profile information.
